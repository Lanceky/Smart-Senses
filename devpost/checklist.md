- [x] **1. The dashboard shows your real headphone status and volume, live, and keeps monitoring after you leave the app**
  Becomes usable: Smart-Senses installs on the Galaxy A03 and opens on the black console dashboard. Tap `START` next to `MONITORING: OFF` to start monitoring.
  - The Ears card shows, updating within a second:
    - whether headphones are connected, and which type;
    - the media volume %;
    - `PLAYING` or `PAUSED`.
  - The top bar shows `MONITORING: ACTIVE`.
  - The System Log shows `monitoring started` and headphone connect and disconnect lines, read from Room.
  - Leaving the app doesn't stop monitoring.
  - Trends, History and Settings show a console placeholder until their steps arrive.
  Why now: setup lives inside this first slice, and it proves the three riskiest foundations together:
  - the AGP 9.1 + built-in Kotlin + KSP/Room + Compose toolchain;
  - a `specialUse` foreground service that survives switching apps on Samsung;
  - that `AudioManager` really reports headphones, volume and playback the way the spec assumes.

  Every later step stands on this.
  PRD ref: `prd.md > Background Monitoring`, `prd.md > Screens and Layout` (Dashboard: top bar, Ears card, Eyes/Device "coming later"), `prd.md > System Log`, `prd.md > Look and Feel`
  Spec ref: `spec.md > Stack`, `spec.md > File Structure`, `spec.md > Look and Feel`, `spec.md > Data Model`. Components: `App Shell and Navigation`, `Monitoring Service`, `Audio Reader`, `Live Status`, `Monitoring Notification`, `Room Database`, `Settings Store (DataStore)`, `Dashboard`, `System Log`, `Theme and Logo` (theme only).
  Build:
  - **Project:** Gradle 9.3.1 wrapper, `libs.versions.toml`, root and app build files (compileSdk/targetSdk 36, minSdk 26), and the manifest (permissions, `specialUse` service, no INTERNET, `allowBackup=false`).
  - **App container:** `SmartSensesApp`.
  - **Room v1:** all three tables and DAOs, generated by KSP.
  - **`SettingsStore`:** all keys.
  - **Theme:** colors, monospace type, and the Console components.
  - **Monitoring:** `AudioReader`, `LiveStatus`, and `MonitoringService` (startForeground first, 1 s loop, connect/disconnect events, START_STICKY, stops when `monitoringEnabled` turns off), plus the notification channel.
  - **Shell:** `MainActivity` (restarts monitoring when opened if it's enabled but not running; `MONITORING: STOPPED` otherwise) and `AppScaffold`.
  - **Dashboard:** top bar with `START`, Ears card, "coming later" cards, System Log panel.
  Verify (mechanical):
  1. `./gradlew assembleDebug` succeeds, including Room code generated by KSP.
  2. `./gradlew installDebug` to the A03.
  3. Launch, then tap `START` using `adb shell input` (found via `uiautomator dump`).
  4. `adb shell dumpsys activity services com.lanceky.smartsenses` shows the service running as a foreground service.
  5. Set the volume with `adb shell cmd media_session volume --stream 3 --set N`. The UI dump shows the new %.
  6. Press Home, wait 30 s: the service is still in the foreground.
  7. The System Log shows `monitoring started`.
  8. Logcat shows no crash.
  Learner check:

- [x] **3. Every warning and listening session waits in History, even after a restart**
  Becomes usable: the History tab lists, newest first:
  - **warnings:** time, `EARS`, volume %, how long, output, `▲ CAUTION` or `■ DANGER`, and a `DEMO` tag;
  - **listening sessions:** start time, peak volume, minutes listened, output, highest severity, and `ongoing` while still open.

  With nothing recorded, it shows a console empty state. Readings are saved every 10 s of listening. Everything survives force-closing the app and restarting the phone.
  Why now: recording is the "bigger picture" half of the kernel, and the data source for Patterns and Trends. Building it right after the card means everything later reads real rows.
  PRD ref: `prd.md > History`, `prd.md > States and Boundaries` (Persistence, First use), `prd.md > Background Monitoring` (paused time never counts)
  Spec ref: Components: `History Screen`, `Room Database`, `Monitoring Service`. Plus `spec.md > Data Model`.
  Build:
  - **Readings:** flushed every 10 s, only when `playingMs > 0`, and also on a disconnect or stop.
  - **Session lifecycle:**
    - Open on connect; add totals on each flush; raise `maxSeverity` on a warning.
    - Close on a disconnect or stop, and delete sessions with no listening.
    - Close stale sessions when monitoring starts.
  - **History screen:** warnings and sessions merged, newest first, with the empty state.
  Verify (mechanical):
  1. `installDebug`.
  2. After playback with headphones, pull `databases/smart-senses.db*` via `adb exec-out run-as com.lanceky.smartsenses cat …` and query it with sqlite3 on the laptop. Readings exist only for playing time; a closed session has the right totals; a paused-only session was deleted.
  3. `adb shell am force-stop com.lanceky.smartsenses`, then relaunch: the History UI dump still lists the rows.
  Learner check:
